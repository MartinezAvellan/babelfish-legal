Last updated: May 2026

AI-Generated Content May Contain Errors

Important Warning

AI-generated translations and outputs may contain inaccuracies, errors, inappropriate content, or factually incorrect information. AI models can "hallucinate" content that appears plausible but is entirely fabricated.

Always review and verify AI-generated content before using it. Do not rely on AI outputs without human verification, especially for important communications, official documents, or situations where accuracy is critical.

Translation Limitations

AI-powered translations may:

  • Miss cultural nuances or context-specific meanings

  • Fail to capture the intended tone or formality level

For critical translations (legal documents, medical information, business contracts), always consult a professional human translator.
