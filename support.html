Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up Babel Fish on your iOS device:

Install Babel Fish

Download and install Babel Fish from the App Store on your iOS device.

Add Your OpenRouter API Key

Open Babel Fish and enter your OpenRouter API key in the settings. You can get an API key from openrouter.ai.

Add the Keyboard

Go to Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard and select Babel Fish.

Allow Full Access

To use AI features (translation and Mini Chat), tap on Babel Fish in the keyboard list and enable Allow Full Access. This is required for the keyboard to connect to AI services.

Start Using Babel Fish

Switch to the Babel Fish keyboard in any app by tapping the globe icon. Use the Translate or Mini Chat features to access AI capabilities.

Troubleshooting

Keyboard not appearing

Make sure you have added Babel Fish in Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards. If it still does not appear, try restarting your device.

Full Access is disabled

AI features require Full Access to connect to the internet. Go to Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Babel Fish and enable "Allow Full Access".

API key not working

Verify that your OpenRouter API key is correct and has available credits. Check your API key at openrouter.ai and ensure it has not expired or been revoked.

Translation or chat not responding

Check your internet connection. Ensure Full Access is enabled for the keyboard. Verify your API key has sufficient credits. Try selecting a different AI model.

Purchase not loading or stuck

Ensure you have a stable internet connection. Try closing and reopening the app. Check that you are signed into your Apple ID in Settings. If the issue persists, restart your device.

Restore Purchases not working

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. Check your internet connection. Try signing out and back into your Apple ID, then attempt the restore again.

Keyboard crashes or freezes

Try removing and re-adding the keyboard in iOS Settings. If the problem continues, reinstall the app from the App Store. Your Pro purchase can be restored after reinstalling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Babel Fish record my typing?

No. Babel Fish only processes text that you explicitly submit through Translate or Mini Chat. We do not monitor, record, or collect your general typing activity.

Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

iOS requires Full Access for keyboard extensions to connect to the internet. This is necessary for AI features that communicate with OpenRouter and AI providers. Without Full Access, AI features will not function.

What happens to my text when I use AI features?

When you use Translate or Mini Chat, your submitted text is sent to OpenRouter, which routes it to your selected AI model provider. These third-party services process your text according to their own privacy policies.

Is my API key secure?

Your API key is stored locally on your device and is only sent to OpenRouter for authentication. We do not send your key to any other servers or share it with third parties.
