Babel Fish Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Babel Fish is an AI-powered keyboard for iOS focused on translation and AI chat.

Information We Process

Babel Fish only processes text explicitly submitted by the user through actions such as Translate or Mini Chat.

API Key Storage

User-provided API keys are stored locally on the user’s device.

Keyboard Data

Babel Fish does not collect background keystrokes and does not continuously monitor user typing.

Third-Party Services

Text may be processed through OpenRouter and selected AI model providers only when explicitly triggered by the user.

Data Sharing

Babel Fish does not sell personal information.
